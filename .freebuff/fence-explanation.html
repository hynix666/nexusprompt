WHY THE FIX STAYS NARROW

One predicate changed. One reader changed. The asymmetry between the two fence readers is recorded, not repaired.

CHANGED — THE MANIFEST READER

extractRuntimeManifest() now keeps the delimiter's trailing text and requires rest.trim() === "" before closing. Failing to close is the safe direction here: an unclosed fence hides more, and hidden content declares nothing.

UNCHANGED — THE USE-SIDE STRIPPER

stripDocumentationSpans() is a port of frozen source consumed by six gates. It closes more eagerly, so it strips less and its gates see more — also the visible-FAIL direction. Unifying the readers would move six gates' verdicts and wants its own ADR.

The contract is intentionally conservative: an ambiguous heading or closer produces a failure someone sees and fixes. An accepted non-manifest is a silent pass nobody ever sees — every rule on this page exists to keep that direction impossible.
